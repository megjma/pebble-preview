# Posting About Freestyle: The 3 Rules

We love that you're sharing Freestyle with your audience. Because we've given you product (or payment), the law treats your posts as Freestyle ads — so here are the three rules that keep us both safe. That's it. Three.

---

## 1. Tell people we're working together — on every post

Put **#ad** (or "Freestyle sent me these to try") at the **top** of your caption, before "…more."
In videos, **say it out loud in the first few seconds** *and* put it on screen.
Do this on every post, Story, and stream — even if you'd recommend us for free, and even if you disclosed yesterday.

Vague doesn't count: #collab, #sp, #ambassador, or just tagging us isn't disclosure.

## 2. Tell YOUR story, in YOUR words

We will never hand you a script — your voice is the whole reason we partnered with you. What happened with *your* baby, said your way, is perfect content: "We made it through the night with zero leaks." "Way fewer blowouts." "Her skin has been so happy since we switched."

The only thing to skip is stating facts or promises that aren't your own experience: no statistics or percentages, no "best/safest diaper," no "never leaks," no comparisons to Pampers, Huggies, or anyone else. Your story doesn't need any of that anyway.

## 3. Skip the danger words

These words create legal problems no matter how true they feel — never use them:

- **prevents / treats / cures / healed** (diaper rash, eczema, anything medical)
- **hypoallergenic** · **chemical-free** · **non-toxic** (in your own words)
- **biodegradable / compostable / eco-friendly / sustainable**
- **safest** · **guaranteed** · **doctor/dermatologist recommended**

Instead of "cured her rash" → say "way less redness since we switched."
Instead of "eco-friendly" → say "the core is made from bamboo."

---

**The fine print (30 seconds):** Only post if you actually use Freestyle and mean what you say. Don't leave reviews on Amazon/Target/etc. in exchange for free product. Real baby, real footage — no AI stand-ins presented as real.

**Not sure about a caption? Send us the draft first — we always say yes or fix it fast.** 💛

*Following these keeps you compliant with FTC rules for sponsored content. The claims and their legal backing are Freestyle's responsibility — your only jobs are the three rules above.*
