## Creator Partnership Agreement

This Creator Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is between **[Freestyle legal entity name]** ("Freestyle," "we") and **[Creator name / business name]** ("Creator," "you"), effective as of the date of last signature.

### 1. The Partnership

Freestyle is engaging you to create and publish social media content featuring Freestyle products, as described in the campaign brief attached in Growi (the "Brief"). The Brief specifies deliverables, platforms, quantities, and deadlines; if this Agreement and the Brief conflict, this Agreement controls.

### 2. Compensation

- **Product:** Freestyle will provide the products listed in the Brief at no cost.
- **Commission:** [X]% of net sales attributed to your tracked link or code, calculated and paid through Growi on [payment schedule], per the payment structure configured in Growi.
- **[Optional] Fee:** $[amount], payable [schedule].
- Compensation is not contingent on the sentiment of your content or on any review, rating, or specific claim. Freestyle will never pay for, or condition anything on, a positive review.

### 3. Your Voice, Your Content

- Content is created in **your own words and style**. Freestyle will not provide scripts or required claim language, and does not require pre-approval of your captions unless the Brief says otherwise.
- Your content must reflect your **honest opinions and your actual experience** using the products with your child. Only participate while you are a genuine user of the products.
- You will not present sponsored content as an independent or impartial review, and will not use AI-generated depictions of product use presented as real.

### 4. FTC Disclosure (required on every post)

You will clearly and conspicuously disclose your relationship with Freestyle on **every** post, story, stream, or other content featuring Freestyle products, in accordance with the FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 C.F.R. Part 255), including:

- a plain-language disclosure (e.g., **#ad**, "Sponsored by Freestyle," "Freestyle sent me these," or "I earn a commission from my link") placed **at the beginning of the caption**, before any truncation; and
- for video content, a **verbal disclosure within the first few seconds** plus on-screen text; and
- use of the platform's built-in branded-content/paid-partnership tool where available, **in addition to** (not instead of) the above.

Receipt of free product or commission eligibility alone triggers this obligation.

### 5. Content Standards

You agree not to state or imply, in any content under this Agreement:

- that any Freestyle product **prevents, treats, cures, or heals** diaper rash, eczema, or any medical condition (statements about your own family's general experience with dryness, comfort, or reduced irritation are fine);
- the terms **"hypoallergenic," "chemical-free," "non-toxic," "all-natural," "biodegradable," "compostable," "eco-friendly," "sustainable," "flushable," "safest,"** or **"guaranteed"**;
- statistics, percentages, test results, or superiority claims (e.g., "the most absorbent diaper"), or comparisons naming other brands;
- that any physician, dermatologist, or other expert recommends the products;
- content that is unlawful, or that disparages competitors on safety grounds.

You also agree to Freestyle's Creator Guidelines attached as **Exhibit A**, which are incorporated into this Agreement. Your signature confirms you have read them.

### 6. Corrections

If Freestyle notifies you that a post does not comply with Sections 4 or 5, you will correct or remove it within **48 hours**. Repeated non-compliance is grounds for termination under Section 10. Freestyle's review of any content is for compliance with this Agreement only and is not legal advice to you.

### 7. Retail Reviews

You will not post ratings or reviews of Freestyle products on retail platforms (e.g., Amazon, Target.com) in connection with this partnership, unless the platform's policies permit incentivized reviews and the review clearly discloses that you received free product or compensation.

### 8. Usage Rights

- You grant Freestyle a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to **repost and share** the deliverables on Freestyle's own organic social channels and website, with credit to you, for [12] months from posting.
- **Paid usage** (ads, whitelisting/partnership ads, marketplace listings): [not included / included for [X] months at $[amount], as configured in the Growi contract].
- You retain ownership of your content. Freestyle may not edit deliverables in ways that change their meaning.

### 9. Independent Contractor; No Exclusivity

You are an independent contractor, not an employee or agent of Freestyle. This Agreement is non-exclusive [unless the Brief specifies a category-exclusivity window: no competing diaper/wipe brand promotions within [X] days of each deliverable].

### 10. Term and Termination

This Agreement runs from the effective date until the deliverables in the Brief are complete [or: for [X] months]. Either party may terminate on written notice (email or Growi message suffices). On termination: earned commissions through the termination date remain payable; Sections 4–8 survive for content already published; and no further deliverables are owed.

### 11. Representations

Each party represents it has the authority to enter this Agreement. You represent that your content will be your original work, will not infringe third-party rights (including music licensing on the applicable platform), and that any person appearing in your content has consented (you consent on behalf of your minor children).

### 12. General

This Agreement (with the Brief and Exhibit A) is the entire agreement for this campaign and is governed by the laws of [State], with disputes resolved in the courts of [County, State]. Neither party may assign it without consent. Amendments require agreement by both parties in writing (Growi contract updates suffice).
