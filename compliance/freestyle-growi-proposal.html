A Creator Program on Growi

Proposal: a 90-day nano/micro creator flood on TikTok and Instagram, run through Growi, built on own-voice content — real parents telling their real Freestyle story in their own words.

TL;DR

For roughly $5,900 in fixed costs plus sales-funded commissions, we recruit ~125 nano/micro parent creators (2–10k followers) with no posting quotas — gift + commission energy — netting ~80–100 active posters and 200–300 pieces of real in-use content per quarter (unboxings don't count). The play is content volume, not reach: we harvest the best-performing posts as ad creative — the model where peer brands like Needed report their best ads coming from sub-5k creators. Guardrails already built.

The idea

What we'd run

  • Platforms: TikTok + Instagram (Reels and Stories) — where parents actually discover diaper brands.
  • Creators: recruit ~125 nano/micro parent creators (2–10k followers) in two cohorts, to net ~80–100 active posters. Small accounts are the point: highest engagement rates on the platform, the most "real mom next door" energy, gift-tier economics — and the tier where peer brands (Needed among them) report their best-performing ad creative originating, often from sub-5k accounts.
  • The deal per creator — the hybrid model: gifted product + affiliate commission, and no posting quota. Posting is voluntary; what keeps the roster honest is that staying in the program (and receiving the next product drop) requires at least one published post showing real product use — posting matters, sentiment never does. On top of commission, a rotating monthly bonus (volume, engagement, or conversion — e.g., "$50 for 3 in-use videos this month," "$100 if a post clears 25k views") keeps the middle of the roster producing; every bonus keys off behavior and metrics, never sentiment. Over-recruiting (~125 to net ~100) is cheaper than enforcement at ~$40 a creator.
  • The content — in use, not unboxed: their own words, their own style — no scripts, no brand-supplied claims. The brief asks for product-in-life content: overnight tests, changing-table routines, diaper-bag reality, sensitive-skin journeys. Unboxing/haul videos don't count toward roster activity or any program metric — they're the lowest-converting creator format and unusable as ad creative. Own-voice is deliberate: it performs better and is the legally safest category (personal experience needs no substantiation file).
  • The funnel math: ~125 recruited × ~60–70% posting (the post-to-stay mechanic plus commission incentives) ≈ 80–100 active creators averaging 2–3 in-use pieces over the quarter → ~200–300 total pieces, all voluntary. The original videos among them (~150+) are the ad-asset pool; Stories build awareness but mostly aren't licensable creative. Extra volume comes from re-upping the best performers with a second product drop, not from quotas.
  • Duration: 90-day pilot, then a go/grow/kill decision on the data — including which follower tier actually produced the winning creative.

Why

What we get out of it

An ad-creative factory, not an influencer campaignEvery deliverable comes with a repost license, with paid-usage (whitelisting/Spark Ads) as a cheap upgrade for winners — and at the 2–10k tier, licensing is far cheaper than with bigger creators. 300–450 authentic pieces per quarter (~200+ original videos) is a full creative-testing pipeline: we find what converts organically, then put media dollars only behind proven content. This is exactly the model behind Needed-style results, where sub-5k creators produced the best ads.
Awareness where parents shopReach into dozens of trusted parent audiences on TikTok/IG — the discovery channel for DTC baby brands.
Social proof at scaleDozens of real families visibly using Freestyle — the trust layer every paid campaign benefits from.
Performance data, not vibesGrowi tracks every creator's clicks and attributed sales, so we learn which creators, angles, and hooks actually convert.
An always-on affiliate armCommission structure means the program keeps selling after the pilot — costs scale only with revenue.
An ambassador benchThe pilot surfaces the 5–10 creators worth deeper retainers and exclusivity later.

Protection

The guardrails (already built)

The FTC holds the brand liable for creator content, so the program ships with a compliance stack — all drafted and ready:

Contract with built-in acknowledgmentOur creator agreement template goes into Growi's e-sign flow with the "3 Rules" guidelines attached as Exhibit A — the signature is the FTC-expected written acknowledgment, no extra process.
Disclosure on every post#ad (or equivalent) at the top of every caption, spoken in the first seconds of video — contractually required, and the platform paid-partnership tools on top.
Own words, no claimsCreators share only their real experience. No statistics, no "prevents rash," no "hypoallergenic/chemical-free/biodegradable," no competitor comparisons — a short danger-words list, not a script. Our campaign briefs deliberately contain no product stats, so there's nothing to misquote.
48-hour correction clauseNon-compliant post → we request a fix → confirmed within 48 hours → logged. Repeat issues end the partnership.
Sample-based spot-checks, scaled for volumeWith ~100 creators we review a rotating sample (~25%/month, every creator at least once during the pilot) — the enforcement evidence regulators look for, kept proportionate. Worth noting: nano creators are the least familiar with FTC rules of any tier, which is exactly why the 3 Rules brief and the e-signed acknowledgment do the heavy lifting at onboarding.
Reviews Rule hygieneNothing is ever conditioned on positive sentiment, and no retail-site reviews in exchange for product — keeping the FTC's ~$50k-per-violation reviews rule fully away from us.

Supporting docs, all drafted: the internal Rulebook, the Responsibility Map (who owns what), the Creator Playbook + 3 Rules brief, and the contract template. Counsel review of the contract is the one outstanding to-do before the first signature.

Measuring it

What success looks like at day 90

  • Content: 200+ published in-use deliverables (150+ original videos) — unboxings excluded from every count; 15–25 assets licensed into paid testing.
  • Ad performance: the headline metric for this model — CTR/CPA of creator-sourced ads vs. our current creative in the same placements. This is where the Needed-style thesis gets proven or disproven with our own data.
  • Performance: attributed revenue vs. commission paid; cost per usable ad asset vs. produced-content benchmark; post rate (target ≥60% of recruited creators publishing at least one in-use piece).
  • Efficiency: which follower band (2–5k vs. 5–10k) and content angle drives the best creative — the input for the scale-up cohort.
  • Compliance: zero uncorrected disclosure misses (spot-check log as evidence).
