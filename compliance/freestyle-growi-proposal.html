A Creator Program on Growi

Proposal: a 90-day pilot creator program on TikTok and Instagram, run through Growi, built on own-voice content — creators telling their real Freestyle story in their own words.

TL;DR

For roughly $2,100 in fixed costs plus sales-funded commissions, we get ~30 parent creators producing 60–90 pieces of real content in a quarter — an always-on awareness engine and a library of authentic creative we can license into paid ads, at a fraction of the cost of a single produced shoot. The compliance guardrails are already built.

The idea

What we'd run

  • Platforms: TikTok + Instagram (Reels and Stories) — where parents actually discover diaper brands.
  • Creators: ~25–30 micro/mid parent creators (roughly 10k–150k followers) to start — high trust, low cost, and their audiences are our exact customer.
  • The deal per creator: gifted product + affiliate commission on their tracked link/code. No flat fees to start; a small fee tier can be added later for proven performers.
  • The content: their own words, their own style — no scripts, no brand-supplied claims. Real overnight tests, diaper-bag routines, sensitive-skin journeys. This is deliberate: own-voice content performs better and is the legally safest category (personal experience needs no substantiation file).
  • Duration: 90-day pilot, then a go/grow/kill decision on the data.

Why

What we get out of it

A creative library we can run as adsEvery deliverable comes with a repost license, with paid-usage (whitelisting/Spark Ads) as an upgrade for the winners. 60–90 authentic pieces per quarter beats one $15k produced shoot for testing ad angles — we find what converts organically, then put media dollars only behind proven content.
Awareness where parents shopReach into dozens of trusted parent audiences on TikTok/IG — the discovery channel for DTC baby brands.
Social proof at scaleDozens of real families visibly using Freestyle — the trust layer every paid campaign benefits from.
Performance data, not vibesGrowi tracks every creator's clicks and attributed sales, so we learn which creators, angles, and hooks actually convert.
An always-on affiliate armCommission structure means the program keeps selling after the pilot — costs scale only with revenue.
An ambassador benchThe pilot surfaces the 5–10 creators worth deeper retainers and exclusivity later.

Protection

The guardrails (already built)

The FTC holds the brand liable for creator content, so the program ships with a compliance stack — all drafted and ready:

Contract with built-in acknowledgmentOur creator agreement template goes into Growi's e-sign flow with the "3 Rules" guidelines attached as Exhibit A — the signature is the FTC-expected written acknowledgment, no extra process.
Disclosure on every post#ad (or equivalent) at the top of every caption, spoken in the first seconds of video — contractually required, and the platform paid-partnership tools on top.
Own words, no claimsCreators share only their real experience. No statistics, no "prevents rash," no "hypoallergenic/chemical-free/biodegradable," no competitor comparisons — a short danger-words list, not a script. Our campaign briefs deliberately contain no product stats, so there's nothing to misquote.
48-hour correction clauseNon-compliant post → we request a fix → confirmed within 48 hours → logged. Repeat issues end the partnership.
Monthly spot-checksA quick monthly review of tagged content against the checklist — the enforcement evidence regulators look for, ~1 hour/month.
Reviews Rule hygieneNothing is ever conditioned on positive sentiment, and no retail-site reviews in exchange for product — keeping the FTC's ~$50k-per-violation reviews rule fully away from us.

Supporting docs, all drafted: the internal Rulebook, the Responsibility Map (who owns what), the Creator Playbook + 3 Rules brief, and the contract template. Counsel review of the contract is the one outstanding to-do before the first signature.

Measuring it

What success looks like at day 90

  • Content: 60+ published deliverables; 10+ assets worth licensing into paid.
  • Performance: attributed revenue vs. commission paid; cost per usable ad asset vs. produced-content benchmark; engagement rate vs. our owned social.
  • Efficiency: which creator tier and content angle drives the best conversion — the input for scaling decisions.
  • Compliance: zero uncorrected disclosure misses (spot-check log as evidence).
