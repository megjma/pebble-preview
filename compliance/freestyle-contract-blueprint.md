# Freestyle Contract Blueprint

**What the creator agreement needs to cover and what compensation should look like — guidelines for the team setting up contracts in Growi, and the spec to hand legal.**

> This is not a contract. It's the requirements list. Legal drafts (or approves) the actual language before the first creator signs; Growi then handles e-signature, payment structure, and records.

---

## Part 1 — What the contract must cover

1. **No posting quota (the hybrid model).** No minimum posting requirement; creators post if and when they want. Staying on the active roster for future product drops requires at least one published post showing *real product use* — an unboxing alone doesn't qualify. **Drafting rule:** define the qualifying post by use, never sentiment — requiring a post is legal; requiring a favorable post violates the FTC Reviews Rule.
2. **Compensation never tied to sentiment.** An explicit line that no compensation, perk, or roster status depends on what the creator says or how positively they say it. This sentence is the Reviews Rule shield (~$50k+/violation) and documents the own-voice design.
3. **FTC disclosure obligation.** Disclosure on every post: plain language (#ad / "Freestyle sent me these" / "I earn a commission from my link") at the top of the caption; spoken in the first seconds of video plus on-screen text; platform paid-partnership tools on top. Free product or commission eligibility alone triggers it.
4. **Content standards + the 3 Rules as a signed exhibit.** The danger-words list in contract form (no prevents/treats/cures, hypoallergenic, chemical-free, non-toxic, biodegradable/eco terms, flushable, safest, guaranteed, expert endorsements, statistics, named-competitor comparisons), with the 3 Rules creator brief attached as an exhibit — so the e-signature doubles as the written guideline acknowledgment the FTC expects.
5. **Correction window.** Non-compliant post → fix or remove within 48 hours; repeat issues end the partnership.
6. **Retail review ban.** No ratings/reviews on retail platforms in connection with the partnership unless the platform permits incentivized reviews and the review discloses the free product.
7. **Usage rights (the whole point).** Non-exclusive license to repost deliverables on Freestyle's organic channels (with credit) for ~12 months; a pre-negotiated flat paid-usage rate in the same contract so licensing winners into ads is one click. Creator keeps ownership; no edits that change meaning. Without this clause, every great video requires chasing permission before it can become an ad.
8. **Independent contractor, non-exclusive.** Avoids employment-law exposure; keeps the offer light enough for nanos to sign.
9. **Term, termination, survival.** Either side can end on written notice (a Growi message counts); earned commissions stay payable; disclosure duties and usage rights *survive* for content already published — without survival, a departing creator could demand content pulled from ads.
10. **Original work & consent.** Original content, no third-party infringement (music licensing per platform), consent from everyone appearing — with creators consenting on behalf of their own minor children.

## Part 2 — What compensation should look like

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| The gift | One product drop (~$40 diapers + wipes) per cohort, no strings. Re-up drops go to creators who posted at least one in-use piece. |
| Commission | 15% of attributed net sales to start (raise to 20% for proven performers), tracked link/code, paid monthly through Growi. Commission is what makes voluntary posting happen. |
| Flat fees | None at the 2–10k tier — product + commission is market standard; fees are for retainer-level ambassadors later. |
| Paid usage | Pre-negotiated flat $150–250 per asset licensed into ads (whitelisting/Spark Ads), baked into every contract. |
| Bonuses (optional) | Sales-milestone bonuses only (e.g., +5% over a GMV threshold — Growi automates this). |

**Compensation must never:** be conditioned on positive sentiment, a good review, or "loving it" (in the contract, Growi settings, or any DM); pay for retail reviews; or penalize honest, less-than-glowing content.

## Part 3 — Setup mechanics in Growi

- One legal-approved **contract template** in Growi's contract flow; per-campaign specifics (commission %, usage window, product list) live in the campaign/brief.
- Attach the **3 Rules brief as the exhibit** so every e-signature is a recorded acknowledgment.
- Keep campaign briefs **free of product statistics** — describe experiences to explore, never claims.
- Retain the signed-contract records — the compliance paper trail.

## Part 4 — Questions for legal

1. **Minors on camera** — consent/publicity language for creators' children appearing in content we later run as ads, state by state.
2. **License scope** — exact wording for organic repost vs. paid usage (placements, edit rights, duration, revocability).
3. **Governing law and venue** — and whether we accept international creators (tax forms, FTC-equivalent rules abroad).
4. **The post-to-stay mechanic** — confirm the sentiment-neutral wording sits safely inside the FTC Reviews Rule.
5. **Termination and survival** — that usage rights and disclosure duties survive for published content.
